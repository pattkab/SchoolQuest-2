**Full description**

School Quest helps learners in Uganda practise what they learn at school. Choose a class from Baby Class to Primary 7 and find class notes, quizzes and past papers.

- Read class notes and revise one topic at a time.
- Try quick quizzes or practise with past papers.
- Explore educational video links and play Kito’s mini quests.
- See your learning progress and revisit topics that need more practice.
- Adjust text size, contrast, motion and sound for a more comfortable experience.
