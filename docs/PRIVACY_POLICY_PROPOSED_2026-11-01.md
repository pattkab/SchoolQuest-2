# SchoolQuest privacy policy — proposed revision
Effective date: 1 November 2026

**Publication note:** This proposed text must be reviewed against the deployed app and published on the existing Google Doc or another verified public privacy-policy URL before it replaces the currently linked version. Do not treat unimplemented features as launched.

School Quest (also branded SCHOOLQUEST) covers https://schoolquestweb.netlify.app and its Android/iOS applications. Operated by Potentia Motus Ventures Ltd. Privacy, support, deletion: motuspotentia@gmail.com. Features vary by platform and deployment stage.

## 1. Information collected
**Accounts and profiles:** names, telephone numbers, email/Google account identifiers where sign-in is available, password hashes (never Google passwords), role, class, school, district, date of birth, sex, guardian name and phone where required. **Learning:** subjects, practice answers, drawings, scores, feedback, attempts, free practice allowance or entitlement claims, XP and streaks. **Teachers/schools:** registration and qualification details, optional national ID where necessary and authorised, classes, attendance, messages and fee records where enabled. **Uploads:** PDFs, notes, images and learning files. **Technical:** cookies, tokens, IP/device/browser/request and error logs managed by hosting/service providers.

**Payments and subscriptions, where enabled:** provider, payment channel, mobile-money payer phone if supplied, plan/product, amount, currency, provider transaction IDs, payment status, verification and subscription entitlements. Yo Payments may process approved web mobile-money collections; Apple In-App Purchase and Google Play Billing may process native digital subscriptions after implementation. We do not need full card numbers to verify provider-managed payments.

**SchoolQuest Store, where enabled:** physical products (such as tablets, school T-shirts and authorised printed textbooks), quantity, server-calculated prices, an adult buyer's name and telephone, delivery location, order status and fulfilment/returns communications. A submitted request is not a completed sale, paid order, stock reservation or automatic subscription award. A proposed 30-day Pro tablet promotion must be verified and approved before activation.

## 2. Use of data
Create and protect accounts; provide curriculum-aligned learning; save, mark and review work; enforce free and paid-access rules; provide progress reports; support authorised teacher/school administration; respond to users; investigate errors and misuse; and, if enabled, initiate, verify and reconcile payments, process adult order enquiries, organise approved deliveries and handle returns/disputes.

## 3. Children, guardians and purchases
Younger learners should use SchoolQuest with an authorised parent, guardian or educator. Collecting a guardian's name/phone alone does not verify consent or authority. Adults must provide authorisation where required, and children must not be asked for a home address in order to buy. Guardians can seek access, correction and deletion. We may verify authority using proportionate information. SchoolQuest currently says it has no advertising SDKs or behavioural advertising; verify this against each released build before publication. We do not sell personal information.

## 4. Sharing and processors
Authorised teachers and school managers access only learner/school records needed for their responsibilities; platform administrators access records required for operations and support. Netlify hosts the website; Google Firebase supports configured authentication and storage; Google Cloud Vertex AI/Gemini processes educational documents, rubrics, answers or drawings for extraction and AI-assisted marking. AI responses may be incorrect and teacher review remains available. Avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive details in uploaded documents and answers.

Once approved and active, Yo Payments may process web mobile-money collections; Google/Apple may process native in-app purchases. Necessary adult buyer and delivery details may be provided to authorised delivery/fulfilment partners for a physical order. Service providers' applicable policies also apply. Data may be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

## 5. Security, location and test data
Data may be handled outside Uganda (including the United States) through configured hosting/AI infrastructure. HTTPS, hashed passwords and access controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee security. Avoid sharing passwords or unnecessary identity documents. **Current staging limitation:** the hosted service is still being prepared for durable production storage; demonstration records may not reliably persist and must not be relied on as official attendance, fee or learner records.

## 6. Retention and choices
We keep data as long as required for account, learning, support, security and applicable legal needs. Where enabled, payment/order/refund/fulfilment records may be retained when needed for accounting, tax, consumer protection, fraud investigation or disputes. We explain specific lawful exceptions and relevant retention periods when responding to requests. Ask to access, correct, export or delete information at motuspotentia@gmail.com. Profile editing and sign-in alternatives depend on the platform. Essential browser session storage maintains sign-in. Accessibility settings may be stored locally; browser/device read-aloud may rely on a speech provider, does not record the microphone for that feature, and excludes typed passwords/forms from page read-aloud.

## 7. Account and data deletion
Email motuspotentia@gmail.com with subject **School Quest account deletion**, identifying the account via registered phone or email, without sending passwords or full identity documents. A guardian can request for a child subject to authority verification. We aim to complete verified requests in 30 days or explain lawful exceptions/delays. Requests cover account, profile and learning data and relevant processors where applicable; independently retained school records or legally required purchase/payment records may need separate handling. Uninstalling does not delete an account. Account deletion does **not** cancel a Play Store or App Store subscription: manage that subscription in the relevant store.

## 8. External links and changes
External learning links and optional provider services have their own terms. We update the effective date and provide appropriate notice/consent for material changes, especially those affecting children.
